Delete Your Ahead Fitness Account

We value your privacy and respect your choice to manage your personal data. If you wish to delete your Ahead Fitness account, please follow the steps below:

1. Send an email to info@aheadfitness.ng with the subject line “Account Deletion Request.”

2. Include your registered email address and (optional) a short note telling us why you’re deleting your account — this helps us improve.

3. Once your request is received, we’ll verify your identity and permanently delete your account and data within 7 business days.

Note:

  • This process is irreversible. All personal data, booking history, and progress records will be removed.
  • If you are an active member, please ensure your subscription is canceled before requesting deletion.

For questions about privacy or data handling, contact our support team anytime at info@aheadfitness.ng

Delete Account on the Mobile App

If you wish to delete your account on our mobile app, please follow the steps below. Deleting your account will remove all your associated data and access to our services.

Step 1: Go to Your Profile:

  • Open the app on your device.
  • Tap the Profile icon located at the bottom right corner of your screen.

Step 2: Open Account Settings:

  • Scroll down to the Settings section.
  • Select Delete Account.

Step 3: Review the Deletion Information:

A confirmation message will appear, explaining what happens when you delete your account. Please note that you will lose access to:

  • Your membership and plan history.
  • Your saved preferences and settings.
  • Your check-in history and progress.
  • Your profile and payment information.

This action cannot be undone.

Step 4: Confirm Deletion:

To proceed:

  • Tap Delete Account (red button).

If you change your mind:

  • Tap Cancel to return to your profile.

Step 5: Email Confirmation:

After you confirm deletion in the app:

  • A deletion confirmation email will be sent to your registered email address.

Step 6: 90-Day Grace Period:

  • Your account enters a 90-day deactivation period.
  • During this period, your profile and data are temporarily inactive but not yet permanently deleted.
  • To retrieve your account you will have to reach out to, where our staff personnel will vet and overview your request.
  • After 90 days, your account and all associated data will be permanently removed from our system and cannot be recovered.

Need Help?

If you experience any issues or wish to recover your account within the grace period, please contact our support team at info@aheadfitness.ng
