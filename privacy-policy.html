Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 July 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Ahead Fitness collects, uses, and protects your personal data when using our mobile app in Nigeria.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number.
  • Health Data: Weight and fitness goals.
  • Usage Data: App activity, log-ins, and device type.
  • Payment Info: Transactions processed via Paystack.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve fitness features.
  • To process your subscription payments.
  • To offer customer support.
  • To send service-related updates and offers.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share data with Paystack for secure payment processing.
  • Data may be shared with third-party service providers helping us operate the App.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You can request to access, update, or delete your personal information by contacting us at info@aheadfitness.ng

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from users under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

info@aheadfitness.ng
