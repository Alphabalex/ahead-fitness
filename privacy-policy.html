Privacy Policy

Ahead Fitness Privacy Policy (V3)

Effective date: 01 October 2026 · Version: 3.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Ahead Fitness Limited ("Ahead Fitness", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Ahead Fitness mobile app, our website aheadfitness.ng, and our services at our branches in Abuja.

Ahead Fitness Limited (RC 8320744), 1, Pall Mall Plaza, Gwarinpa, Abuja, is the data controller for this data. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at info@aheadfitness.ng.

If you are a guest invited by a member, section 3 explains what we collect about you.

1. Information we collect

Category What it includes Where it comes from
Account and contact Name, email, phone number, date of birth, sex, home branch You, at sign-up or at the front desk
Profile photo A photo taken with your phone camera in the app You. Staff see it when you check in
Membership and check-ins Plan, start and end dates, branch, date and time of each check-in Our systems, when you scan your QR code
Body composition Weight, height, body fat, visceral fat, muscle and lean mass by body segment, fitness score and scan history Body composition scans you take at a branch
Food, dietary and allergy information Allergies and dietary needs you declare for meal plans and orders You
Goals Fitness goals you set in the app You
Orders and delivery Items ordered, delivery address, delivery notes You
Wallet, points and payments Wallet top-ups and spend, points earned and used, transaction records. We do not store your full card details You, our systems and our payment processor
Referrals Your referral code and who joined using it Our systems
Guests A guest's name and phone number, and the date of their visit The member who invites them, and the guest when they verify by OTP
Device and usage Device type, operating system, app version, logins, crash reports, pages and features used Your device and the app
Website IP address, browser type, pages visited and cookie data Your browser (see section 6)
Communications Messages you send us and support requests You

We do not collect exit times from the gym, and we do not use facial recognition.

3. Health data, recommendations and guests

Health data. Body composition results, allergies and dietary needs are sensitive personal data under the NDPA. We ask for your explicit consent before we process them, the first time you take a scan or set up a meal plan. You can withdraw consent in the app in the profile section or by emailing us. If you withdraw it, we stop showing results and personalised recommendations, but your membership continues.

Results are estimates. Body composition figures and the bands we show (for example low, borderline, healthy) are estimates from the scanner and our own reference ranges. They are not a medical diagnosis.

Recommendations. The app suggests meals, classes and products based on your latest scan and goals. This is automated, but it never decides your access, price or eligibility for anything.

Guests. When a member invites you, we receive your name and phone number. We send you a one-time code to verify, record the date of your visit, and keep your number for 90 days so that the same guest is not invited again within that period. We do not use your details for marketing. You can ask us to delete them by emailing us at info@aheadfitness.ng.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers below, under written agreements that require them to protect it and use it only on our instructions.

Provider type Why
Payment processor Card and bank payments
Maps and distance Delivery fees and addresses
Body composition scanning Running scans and returning results
Cloud hosting and database Storing app data
SMS, OTP and messaging Verification codes and service messages
Push notifications, analytics and crash reports Sending alerts and fixing bugs
Delivery riders Delivering orders (name, phone, address only)

Our staff see only what they need. For example, front-desk staff see your photo and membership status at check-in, and kitchen staff see allergy notes on your order.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or restructuring of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

5. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our providers store or process data outside Nigeria (e.g. UK, USA). Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows, such as adequacy decisions, contractual clauses or your consent, and we make sure your data gets a comparable level of protection.

6. Cookies, analytics and marketing

Our website uses essential cookies to work. We use analytics or advertising cookies only if you accept them in the cookie banner. In the app, crash reporting run by default.

We send offers by WhatsApp, SMS, email or push only if you have opted in. Every marketing message tells you how to opt out, and you can also change this at Settings > Notifications. Service messages about your membership, orders and payments are not marketing and will still be sent.

7. How long we keep it

Data How long
Account, profile photo, goals, body composition scans While your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of account deletion
Check-in history 24 months, then aggregated or deleted
Allergy and dietary information While your account is open, or until you remove it
Payments, wallet and points ledger, orders 6 years after the transaction, for tax and accounting
Guest details 90 days after the visit
Support messages 24 months
Website and app usage data 13 months

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls limited by staff role, and encryption at rest, regular backups and audit logs. No system is completely secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can:

  • Get a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to how we use it, including for marketing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what we did before
  • Not be subject to a decision based only on automated processing that significantly affects you
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng)

To use these rights, email info@aheadfitness.ng. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account yourself in the app at Profile > Delete account. If you no longer have the app, email us at info@aheadfitness.ng. Deleting your account ends app access. Any unused wallet balance and points are handled as set out in the Terms of Use. Records we must keep by law are retained as shown in section 7.

10. Children

The app and our online services are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes and contact

We will tell you about material changes to this policy in the app and by email before they take effect. Where a change affects how we use health data, we will ask for your consent again.

Questions or requests: info@aheadfitness.ng
